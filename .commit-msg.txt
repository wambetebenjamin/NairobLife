Stop flattening the CBD: the height estimate had a cap that forbade tall buildings

Audit of the baked zone: 3,072 buildings, only 251 (8%) with a height that OSM actually
gives. The other 2,821 are estimated, and their distribution was

  median 11.0 m   p90 17.8 m   max 34.8 m   (zero above 60 m)

while the 251 real ones ran median 34.8 m, max 140 m, 49 above 60 m. That cliff is the
skyline error. The city was a low plateau with towers spiking out of it.

Two causes, both measured
-------------------------
1. MAX_GUESSED_SLENDERNESS was 1.25 -- a hard cap of 1.25x height/sqrt(footprint area) on
   any guess. Against the 251 buildings with real heights, slenderness has a median of 1.35;
   138 of 251 exceed 1.25; among those over 60 m the median is 3.04. The cap made it
   arithmetically impossible for a guessed building to be tall. Raised to 3.2, the p90 of the
   measured set, which still stops a 150 m2 kiosk outline becoming a tower.

2. Untagged buildings with no type fell back to a 1.6..9 floor ceiling. Raised, and a dense
   core now inherits a fraction of the LOCAL tagged median instead of a generic "2 floors"
   tone. Footprint area turned out to be useless as a signal -- Pearson r = -0.024 on log
   area against height, r^2 = 0.001 -- because a 150 m2 tower block and a 5,000 m2 warehouse
   are the same size on a map. Locality is the only honest predictor available here.

   A fixed type table still wins where OSM provides one: a cathedral is not a tower because
   it sits downtown, and a kiosk is not a tower because its neighbours are.

Result
------
  estimated   median 11.0 -> 24.6 m   max 34.8 -> 72.2 m
  real        unchanged (OSM is the source of truth and is not touched)

Jitter is now applied AFTER the slenderness cap. Applied before, every tall building in the
core clamped to the same value and a street came out as one height repeated.

Tests: the old "never guesses a tower onto a small footprint" test hardcoded 1.25, i.e. it
encoded the assumption being disproved, so it was rewritten against the measured p90 plus a
"still nothing absurd" bound. A new test asserts a tall core stays tall and a low edge stays
low, which is the property the old cap was silently destroying.

376 tests, 39 files, lint and format clean.

Still not actual: 2,821 buildings are still guesses, flagged `est`. 298 of them are NAMED
landmarks with no height in OSM at all -- Kenya House Complex, Uniafric House, College House,
National House, Ellis House, 20th Century Plaza, Holy Family Basilica. Fixing those properly
needs `building:levels` from a network-connected Overpass fetch, not a better estimator.
