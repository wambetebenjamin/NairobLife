Add `npm run osm:audit`: how much of the city is observed, and how much is ours

The bake has always flagged each building it had to guess (`est: 1`), but nothing ever
summarised it, so there was no way to see the ratio at a glance. This prints it per zone:
real versus estimated heights, the tallest things in the skyline and whether they are real,
the named landmarks that carry no height, and the tree species split.

    nairobi-cbd
      buildings 3072   real height 251 (8%)   estimated 2821   named 437
      tallest 140 m REAL Times Tower ...
      named landmarks with no height in OSM: 292
      trees 271   with a species or leaf type 43   unknown 228

It immediately surfaced something worth knowing: KICC, the building at the world origin
(WORLD_ORIGIN is KICC), exists in OSM as three separate buildings. Only the 105 m convention
tower carries a real height; the 58.6 m slab and the amphitheatre are both guesses. The
landmark itself was correct -- LANDMARKS references geometry, not heights -- but the number
printed in the audit is the honest one.

An earlier count said 298 named buildings had no height; that was counted against the raw
OSM extract, before the bake merges 3D parts back into outlines and drops some duplicates.
292 is the number that survives into the baked zone, so 292 is what this reports.
